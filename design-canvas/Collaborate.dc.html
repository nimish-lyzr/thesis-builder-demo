Section 4 of 6

Market Gaps

Where meaningful whitespace remains

Two gaps survive the screen. Both sit above the compute layer, where enterprise buying is moving, and neither has a credible incumbent.✓

Agent Identity & Access. As enterprises deploy hundreds of autonomous agents, controlling agent identity, permissions and cross-agent communication is emerging as an unsolved infrastructure problem. Three companies address it directly; none has passed Series A.{{pinLabel}}

Added in version 2. Governance of agent behaviour — who approved an action, under what policy, and whether it can be audited afterwards — is raised repeatedly by enterprise clients and is treated here as a first-order gap rather than a feature of identity.

Cross-Agent Governance. Once several agents act on the same systems, the unresolved question is not capability but accountability. No company in the universe treats this as its primary product.
